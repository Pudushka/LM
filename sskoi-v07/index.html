Before you order

Do the stems need water?

No. Display SSKOI faux stems dry. The printed paper gift box is a dry display vase too.

Is a vase included?

Only the eight-stem Rose Gift Box includes one, its own folding paper vase. Ceramic and glass vessels in the room pictures are styling props.

How do I keep them looking good?

Dust petals and leaves with a soft, dry brush. Keep the stems away from damp areas, heaters and prolonged direct sun.
