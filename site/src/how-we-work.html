05 / Questions05 / 常见问题

Before we start.开始之前。

Do you buy properties outside Hudson County?你们会收购哈德逊县以外的物业吗？

Our current priority is Union City, Jersey City, Weehawken and North Bergen. We will look at nearby New Jersey markets when a property is a strong fit.目前重点是 Union City、Jersey City、Weehawken 与 North Bergen。若物业非常合适，我们也会考虑新泽西州的周边市场。

Is there a minimum investment?有最低投资额吗？

Minimums are set project by project and stated in the offering documents.最低投资额按项目设定，并在发行文件中载明。

Do I need to be an accredited investor?我必须是合格投资者吗？

Private offerings are generally limited to accredited investors. We will confirm eligibility before sharing offering documents.私募项目通常仅面向合格投资者。在提供发行文件前，我们会确认您的资格。

Can I visit a project?可以参观项目吗？

Yes. Site visits are part of evaluation and are welcome for committed partners during renovation.可以。实地查看是评估的一部分，施工期间也欢迎已确定合作的伙伴参观。
