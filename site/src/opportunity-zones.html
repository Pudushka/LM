01 / HOW OZ 2.0 WORKS01 / OZ 2.0 如何运作

Three advantages.
Three moments in time.三重税务优势，
落在三个时间点。

Sell a business, stock, real estate or another appreciated asset, and the gain can be reinvested into a qualified opportunity fund within 180 days. Under the new rules, that one decision unfolds over a decade.出售企业、股票、房地产或其他增值资产后，可在 180 天内将资本利得投入合格机会基金（QOF）。在新规则下，这一个决定将在十年中逐步兑现价值。

2027

  1. 01 · INVEST & DEFER01 · 投资并递延

    Pay nothing on the gain for five years.五年内，资本利得暂不缴税。

    Federal tax on the gain you invest is deferred for five years from the date of investment — a rolling clock, not a fixed deadline. Invest in early 2027 and that tax is not due until 2032.投入 QOF 的资本利得，其联邦税自投资日起递延五年 —— 这是滚动计算的时钟，而非固定截止日。2027 年初投资，相关税款将递延至 2032 年。

  2. 02 · STEP UP02 · 基础上调

    Then pay on 10% less of it.届时，只需就 90% 的利得纳税。

    Hold for five years and the deferred gain may receive a 10% step-up in basis — so tax is owed on 90% of it. Qualified rural funds may receive a 30% step-up.持有满五年，递延利得的计税基础可上调 10%，即仅就 90% 纳税。合格农村机会基金可获 30% 上调。

  3. 03 · EXCLUDE03 · 增值免税

    Then the appreciation may be tax-free.此后，增值部分可免缴联邦税。

    Hold for at least ten years and the appreciation on the investment may be excluded from federal capital gains tax entirely — for gains through the 30th year of the investment.持有至少十年，投资的增值部分可完全免除联邦资本利得税；免税适用于投资后 30 年内产生的增值。

Investors must satisfy a number of requirements, including minimum holding periods, to qualify for opportunity zone tax benefits. Deferred tax is not forgiven and becomes due at a later date. Consult your own tax advisor.投资者须满足包括最短持有期在内的多项要求，方可享受机会区税收优惠。递延税款并非免除，将于之后到期缴纳。请咨询您的税务顾问。

    10 / Glossary10 / 术语表

    The vocabulary, in plain words.用平实的语言，读懂术语。

    QOF Qualified opportunity fund合格机会基金
    A corporation or partnership organized to invest in opportunity zone property. Investors put eligible gains into a QOF to receive the tax benefits.为投资机会区资产而设立的公司或合伙企业。投资者将合格利得投入 QOF 以享受税收优惠。
    QOZB Qualified opportunity zone business合格机会区企业
    An operating business or property company in a zone, usually held by a QOF, in which substantially all tangible property is used in the zone.位于机会区内、通常由 QOF 持有的经营企业或物业公司，其绝大部分有形资产在区内使用。
    Substantial improvement实质性改善
    For an existing building, investing more than its purchase basis (excluding land) within 30 months. Rural zones under OZ 2.0 use a 50% threshold.对于既有建筑，须在 30 个月内投入超过其购入基础（不含土地）。OZ 2.0 下农村机会区的门槛为 50%。
    180-day period180 天期限
    The window to invest an eligible gain in a QOF, generally starting on the date the gain is recognized.将合格利得投入 QOF 的期限，一般自利得确认之日起算。
    Basis step-up基础上调
    An increase in the tax basis of the investment, which reduces the deferred gain that is eventually taxed. OZ 2.0 provides 10% after five years, or 30% for qualified rural funds.投资计税基础的提高，从而减少最终需纳税的递延利得。OZ 2.0 规定持有满五年上调 10%，合格农村基金为 30%。
    Ten-year exclusion十年免税
    After holding a QOF investment for at least ten years, an investor may elect to step basis up to fair market value, so appreciation is not taxed on a sale.持有 QOF 投资满十年后，投资者可选择将基础调至公允市值，出售时增值部分不再征税。
    90% asset test90% 资产测试
    A QOF must hold at least 90% of its assets in opportunity zone property, measured at semiannual testing dates.QOF 须将至少 90% 的资产投于机会区资产，按半年度测试日计算。
    Original use原始用途
    Property whose first use in the zone begins with the fund, such as new construction. Original-use property does not need to meet the substantial-improvement test.在机会区内首次投入使用始于该基金的资产，例如新建项目。原始用途资产无需满足实质性改善测试。
    Accredited investor合格投资者
    An SEC category based on income, net worth or professional credentials. Most private real estate offerings are limited to accredited investors.美国证监会根据收入、净资产或专业资质划定的投资者类别。大多数私募房地产项目仅面向合格投资者。
    Low-income community低收入社区
    The census-tract test used to designate zones. OZ 2.0 tightens it and removes eligibility for contiguous tracts.用于划定机会区的人口普查区标准。OZ 2.0 收紧了该标准，并取消相邻地块的资格。

    No terms match.没有匹配的术语。

    11 / QUESTIONS11 / 常见问题

    Before the first call.沟通之前，您可能想知道。

    Which gains can be invested?哪些利得可以投资？

    Capital gains from many appreciated assets may be eligible — real estate, stock, a business sale, and gains passed through on a Schedule K-1, among others. Only the gain needs to be invested, not the full sale proceeds.多种增值资产产生的资本利得都可能符合条件 —— 包括房地产、股票、出售企业，以及通过 K-1 传递的利得等。只需投入利得部分，而非全部出售所得。

    What happens if I sell before ten years?如果持有不满十年就出售呢？

    The deferred gain is recognized when the investment is sold, or at five years if earlier. Appreciation is excluded from federal tax only after a ten-year hold.递延利得将在出售投资时确认，或在满五年时确认（以较早者为准）。增值部分仅在持有满十年后方可免缴联邦税。

    Who can invest with Lensho Capital?谁可以与 Lensho Capital 一起投资？

    Any opportunity zone offering would be made only to eligible — generally accredited — investors, through definitive offering documents. This page is not an offer.任何机会区投资均仅面向符合条件的投资者（通常为合格投资者），并以正式发行文件为准。本页面不构成要约。

    Can I invest only part of my gain?可以只投资部分利得吗？

    Yes. Benefits apply to the portion of the gain invested within the 180 days. Any amount above the gain does not receive opportunity zone treatment.可以。优惠适用于 180 天内投入的那部分利得；超出利得的投入部分不享受机会区待遇。

    Why does renovation matter so much?为什么改造如此重要？

    An existing building generally qualifies only if the fund substantially improves it within 30 months. That makes renovation planning, budgeting and execution central to whether an investment keeps its tax benefits. Try the planner.既有建筑通常只有在基金于 30 个月内对其进行实质性改善后才符合资格。因此，改造的规划、预算与执行直接关系到投资能否保有税收优惠。试用规划工具。

    Does my state follow the federal rules?州税是否遵循联邦规则？

    Not always. State conformity to opportunity zone treatment varies and can change. Confirm with your tax advisor.不一定。各州对机会区税务处理的衔接程度不同，且可能变化。请与您的税务顾问确认。
