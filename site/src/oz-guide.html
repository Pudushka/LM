01

The basics基本概念

An opportunity zone is a census tract designated for investment because it meets a low-income test. Congress created the program in 2017 to draw private capital into those communities by offering tax benefits to investors who reinvest capital gains there.机会区是因符合低收入标准而被划定用于投资的人口普查区。美国国会于 2017 年设立这一计划，通过向在当地再投资资本利得的投资者提供税收优惠，吸引私人资本进入这些社区。

Investors do not buy property in a zone directly. They invest an eligible gain in a qualified opportunity fund (QOF), a partnership or corporation that in turn owns property or businesses in the zone and must keep at least 90% of its assets there.投资者并不直接购买机会区内的物业，而是将合格利得投入合格机会基金（QOF）。QOF 是合伙企业或公司，由其持有区内的物业或企业，并须将至少 90% 的资产投于区内。

In 2025 the program was rewritten and made permanent. The new version, commonly called OZ 2.0, applies to investments from January 1, 2027, with a new map of zones designated in ten-year rounds.2025 年，该计划经改写并成为永久政策。新版本通常称为 OZ 2.0，适用于 2027 年 1 月 1 日起的投资，并以十年为一轮重新划定机会区。

02

The 180-day clock180 天时钟

An eligible gain must be invested in a QOF within 180 days. For most sales, the clock starts on the date the gain is recognized, usually the closing or trade date.合格利得须在 180 天内投入 QOF。对大多数交易而言，时钟自利得确认之日起算，通常为交割日或交易日。

Some gains start the clock differently:部分利得的起算方式有所不同：

  • Partnership and S-corporation gains reported on a Schedule K-1 can generally start the 180 days on the last day of the entity's tax year, or the due date of its return, rather than the sale date.合伙企业和 S 公司利得（通过 K-1 申报）一般可以实体纳税年度的最后一天或其申报截止日作为 180 天的起算日，而非交易日。
  • Installment sales can start a new 180-day period for each payment received.分期付款销售的每一笔收款都可开启新的 180 天期限。
  • Section 1231 gains from business property have their own timing rules.营业用资产产生的第 1231 条利得适用单独的时间规则。

Why the timing matters this year今年的时间安排为何关键

A gain recognized on or after July 5, 2026 has a 180-day window that reaches into 2027. Investing on or after January 1 may place that gain under the enhanced OZ 2.0 rules. Check your date.2026 年 7 月 5 日或之后确认的利得，其 180 天窗口可延伸至 2027 年。在 1 月 1 日或之后投资，可能使该利得适用 OZ 2.0 的增强规则。测算您的日期。

03

Three benefits, worked through三重优惠示例

OZ 2.0 offers three federal tax benefits, each tied to a holding period. The example below follows a $1,000,000 long-term gain from a sale in November 2026, invested in a QOF on February 1, 2027. It assumes today's top federal rate of 23.8% and that the rate does not change.OZ 2.0 提供三项联邦税收优惠，各自对应一个持有期。以下示例为：2026 年 11 月出售资产产生 100 万美元长期资本利得，于 2027 年 2 月 1 日投入 QOF。假设适用当前最高联邦税率 23.8%，且税率不变。

1. Deferral1. 递延

Tax on the invested gain is deferred until the earlier of a sale of the QOF investment or five years after the investment date. In the example, the gain is recognized on February 1, 2032 instead of in 2026.投入利得的税款递延至出售 QOF 投资之日或投资满五年之日（以较早者为准）。在本例中，利得于 2032 年 2 月 1 日确认，而不是在 2026 年。

2. A 10% step-up2. 10% 基础上调

After five years the investor's basis increases by 10% of the deferred gain, so only 90% is taxed. Investments in qualified rural opportunity funds receive a 30% step-up.持有满五年后，投资者的计税基础提高递延利得的 10%，因此仅 90% 需纳税。投资于合格农村机会基金可获 30% 上调。

3. Tax-free appreciation after ten years3. 十年后增值免税

After ten years the investor can elect to step the basis of the QOF investment up to its fair market value when it is sold, so the appreciation is not taxed. Under OZ 2.0, appreciation is excluded for gains through the 30th year of the investment.持有满十年后，投资者可在出售时选择将 QOF 投资的基础调至公允市值，因此增值部分无需纳税。在 OZ 2.0 下，免税适用于投资后 30 年内的增值。

Point in time时间点Without OZ不使用 OZWith OZ 2.0使用 OZ 2.0

February 2032 (year 5)2032 年 2 月（第 5 年）—$214,200 on $900,000（按 90 万美元）
Sale in 2037 at $1.8M (year 10)2037 年以 180 万美元出售（第 10 年）Tax on appreciation增值部分需纳税$0 on $800,000 appreciation（80 万美元增值）

The largest benefit usually comes from the third step: appreciation over ten or more years that is never taxed. The deferral and step-up matter, but the deferred tax still has to be paid in year five, typically from funds outside the investment.最大的优惠通常来自第三步：十年或更长时间内的增值永不征税。递延与上调同样重要，但递延的税款仍需在第五年缴纳，通常需要用投资以外的资金支付。

04

What changed from OZ 1.0与 OZ 1.0 的差异

  1. Permanent. The program no longer has an end date. New zones are designated every ten years.永久化。计划不再设截止日期，每十年重新划定机会区。
  2. Rolling deferral. Deferral runs five years from each investment, instead of ending on a fixed date (December 31, 2026 under OZ 1.0).滚动递延。递延自每笔投资起算五年，而不是在固定日期结束（OZ 1.0 为 2026 年 12 月 31 日）。
  3. One step-up, available again. A 10% step-up after five years (30% for rural funds) replaces the 10% and 15% step-ups that new OZ 1.0 investors could no longer reach.重新可得的基础上调。满五年上调 10%（农村基金 30%），取代了 OZ 1.0 后期新投资者已无法获得的 10% 与 15% 上调。
  4. A 30-year horizon. The exclusion covers appreciation through the 30th year of the investment.30 年期限。免税覆盖投资后 30 年内的增值。
  5. A tighter map. A stricter income test, no contiguous tracts and exclusion of higher-income tracts focus designations on communities that need investment.更聚焦的版图。更严格的收入标准、取消相邻地块、排除高收入地块，使划定更聚焦真正需要投资的社区。
  6. More reporting. Funds and the businesses they own face expanded information reporting, which rewards sponsors with disciplined administration.更多披露。基金及其持有企业面临更广泛的信息申报要求，管理规范的发起人更具优势。

05

Substantial improvement实质性改善

A fund cannot simply buy an existing building in a zone and wait. Unless the property's original use begins with the fund, the building must be substantially improved: within any 30-month period after acquisition, the fund must add more to the building's basis than the building's basis at purchase. Land is excluded on both sides of the test.基金不能只是买下区内既有建筑然后等待。除非该物业的原始用途始于该基金，否则建筑必须获得实质性改善：在收购后的任意 30 个月内，基金对建筑新增的基础须超过购入时的建筑基础。测试两端均不计土地。

Example示例

A building is bought for $1,200,000, with 30% allocated to land. The building basis is $840,000, so the fund must invest more than $840,000 in the building within 30 months, about $28,000 a month on average.以 120 万美元购入一栋建筑，其中 30% 分摊为土地。建筑基础为 84 万美元，因此基金须在 30 个月内对建筑投入超过 84 万美元，平均每月约 2.8 万美元。

Spend that typically counts includes structural work, building systems, interiors and other capitalized improvements to the building. A written plan and a schedule matter. Businesses held by a fund can also use a working-capital safe harbor that allows cash to be deployed under a written plan over up to 31 months.通常可计入的支出包括结构工程、建筑系统、室内改造以及其他资本化的建筑改善。书面计划与施工进度至关重要。基金持有的企业还可使用营运资金安全港，在书面计划下最长 31 个月内投放资金。

For a sponsor focused on vacant buildings and major renovation, the test is not a hurdle to work around. It describes the work.对于专注空置建筑与大幅翻修的发起人而言，这项测试不是需要绕开的障碍，它描述的正是工作本身。

Under OZ 2.0, buildings in rural zones need improvements of only 50% of basis. Try the improvement planner.在 OZ 2.0 下，农村机会区的建筑只需改善基础的 50%。试用实质改善规划工具。

06

Risks and trade-offs风险与取舍

Tax benefits do not make a weak project a good investment. Consider each of these before investing:税收优惠并不能让一个薄弱的项目变成好投资。投资之前，请逐一考虑以下几点：

  • Illiquidity. The main benefit requires a ten-year hold, and private fund interests are hard to sell early.流动性低。主要优惠须持有十年，私募基金份额很难提前出售。
  • A tax bill without a sale. The deferred gain is taxed at year five whether or not the investment has produced cash.未出售也需缴税。无论投资是否产生现金，递延利得都将在第五年征税。
  • Project risk. Construction costs, approvals, lease-up and interest rates can all move against a plan. Returns are not guaranteed and capital can be lost.项目风险。建造成本、审批、出租进度与利率都可能偏离计划。回报没有保证，本金可能亏损。
  • Compliance risk. Missing the 90% asset test or the substantial-improvement test can cost penalties or the benefits themselves.合规风险。未通过 90% 资产测试或实质性改善测试，可能导致罚款甚至丧失优惠。
  • Rule and rate changes. Treasury guidance, tax rates and state treatment can change over a ten-year hold.规则与税率变化。在十年持有期内，财政部指引、税率及州税处理都可能变化。

Questions to ask a sponsor向发起人提出的问题

  1. Is the property in a zone designated under the OZ 2.0 map, and for how long does that designation run?物业是否位于 OZ 2.0 新版图划定的机会区内？划定期限多长？
  2. Is the building original-use or does it need substantial improvement? What is the budget against the basis, and the schedule against 30 months?建筑属于原始用途，还是需要实质性改善？预算与建筑基础相比如何？进度与 30 个月期限相比如何？
  3. How is the fund structured to meet the 90% asset test, and who prepares compliance reporting?基金如何架构以满足 90% 资产测试？由谁负责合规申报？
  4. What is the expected hold, and how will an exit after year ten work?预期持有期多长？十年后如何退出？
  5. Are distributions planned before year five to help with the deferred tax?第五年之前是否计划分配，以协助支付递延税款？
  6. How much of its own capital is the sponsor investing, and how are fees and promotes structured?发起人自身投入多少资本？费用与超额收益分成如何安排？
  7. What is the sponsor's renovation track record in this market?发起人在该市场的改造业绩如何？
  8. What reporting will investors receive, and how often?投资者将收到哪些报告？频率如何？

How Lensho approaches OZ 2.0Lensho 如何参与 OZ 2.0

Lensho Capital acquires and substantially renovates vacant and underused buildings in Hudson County, New Jersey, primarily in projects under US$2 million. That is the kind of work the substantial-improvement test was written for.Lensho Capital 在新泽西州哈德逊县收购并大幅翻修空置与低效利用的建筑，项目规模主要在 200 万美元以下。这正是实质性改善测试所针对的工作类型。

We are reviewing tracts in Union City, Jersey City, Weehawken and North Bergen as the new map takes effect, and planning projects around the 30-month test from the first site visit. Any offering would be made only to eligible investors through definitive documents.随着新版图生效，我们正在梳理 Union City、Jersey City、Weehawken 与 North Bergen 的地块，并从第一次实地查看起就围绕 30 个月测试规划项目。任何投资机会仅通过正式文件向符合条件的投资者提供。
