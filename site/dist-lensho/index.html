The opportunity zone program, rewritten.
Substantial renovation at its core.机会区计划，全面改写。
以大幅翻修为核心。

From January 1, 2027, eligible capital gains invested in a qualified opportunity fund get a rolling five-year deferral, a 10% step-up and tax-free appreciation after ten years. Rehabilitating existing buildings is central to how the program works, and to how we work.自 2027 年 1 月 1 日起，投入合格机会基金的资本利得可享五年滚动递延、10% 计税基础上调，以及持有十年后的增值免税。改造既有建筑是这一计划的核心，也正是我们的工作方式。

What we consider我们如何看待项目

01

Design & condition空间与建筑状况

Consider the layout, building systems and opportunities to improve function. Understand existing conditions and the practical scope of renovation before shaping a plan.从空间布局、建筑系统和使用功能出发，理解现有条件与改善空间，在形成方案前评估实际的翻修范围。

02

The full project看清项目全貌

Evaluate acquisition cost, renovation scope, financing, carrying costs and reserves together. Consider approvals, construction timing and local demand when testing feasibility.结合收购成本、改造范围、融资、持有成本与储备资金评估项目，并将审批、施工周期和当地需求纳入可行性判断。

03

Aligned interests利益一致

03 / How a project moves03 / 项目如何推进

From a vacant building
to a long-term asset.从一栋空置建筑，
到一项长期资产。

Every project follows the same five stages. Partners know where a project stands, what happens next and what is expected of them.每个项目都经历相同的五个阶段。合作伙伴清楚项目所处阶段、下一步计划，以及各自需要承担的工作。

  1. 01

    Source寻找项目

    Off-market introductions and listings in our priority markets.在重点市场获取场外介绍与公开挂牌项目。

  2. 02

    Evaluate评估

    Site visit, condition review, scope estimate and full-project underwriting.实地查看、状况评估、改造范围估算及全项目测算。

  3. 03

    Structure搭建架构

    Capital stack, partner roles, governance and the documents that record them.资本结构、合作方职责、治理机制及相关文件。

  4. 04

    Renovate改造

    Approvals, contractors, budget control and regular progress reporting.审批、施工方管理、预算控制与定期进度汇报。

  5. 05

    Hold & refinance持有与再融资

    Lease-up, stabilization, refinance and a long-term hold or planned exit.出租、稳定运营、再融资，以及长期持有或按计划退出。
