# 配方照片拍摄清单（给 Codex）v10

每套出两张：`Hero 4:5`（2000 × 2500）和 `Wide 16:9`（2560 × 1440），sRGB JPEG，文件名 `SSKOI_Recipe-<No>_<Name>_<Hero-4x5|Wide-16x9>_v01_REVIEW.jpg`。上传到 Drive `SSKOI_Arrangement-Recipes_v10_REVIEW_20261117/02_Photos`，状态 REVIEW。

## 所有照片的共同要求

- 写实室内大片，水准对标 Architectural Digest、Hermès 和 Louis Vuitton 的居家画册：自然光单侧照明，柔和真实的阴影，材质真实（石灰墙、橡木、胡桃木、石材、亚麻），色调安静，衬托花而不抢花。
- **花必须和参考商品图一致**：花型、花瓣层次、颜色、叶子、花苞数量都按参考图。Austin 玫瑰是参考图里的杯状花型，不要画成开敞露出黄色花蕊的样子；Classic 玫瑰每枝一朵开花加两个花苞；露莲每枝两朵花加一个花苞，叶子是银绿色植绒叶；郁金香是闭合的杯形。
- **枝数必须和配方完全一致**，不加任何配方外的花或绿叶（04 的尤加利是套装的一部分），所有花枝都插在花器里，看得到的花头数量对得上。
- 花器按配方的类型和高宽比；花器不出现品牌或文字。礼盒的纸花器必须是参考图里的深绿植物印花纸盒。
- 不出现人、宠物、文字、Logo、价格；不做拼贴，不用抠图。
- 构图：4:5 里花和花器占画面高度 45–60%，房间要看得出是哪种空间；16:9 留出左侧或右侧三分之一的安静背景，网页会把配方卡放在旁边。
- 每张出图后，和参考图并排检查花型、颜色和枝数，不符的标 NEEDS-FIX，不要交付。

## 逐套清单

### 01 The Long Table（Dining · Blush · 10 枝）

- 1 × Austin Roses · Baby Pink（6 stems；ASIN B0DTFNXBHF）。参考：ssko-v072-austin-baby-pink-set.webp, ssko-v073-austin-baby-pink-detail.webp
- 1 × Flocked Ranunculus · Light Peach（4 stems；ASIN B0DTYG3SY1）。参考：ssko-v072-peony-light-peach-set.webp, ssko-v073-peony-light-peach-detail.webp
- 花器：Low footed stoneware bowl with a flower frog，高约 11 cm，口径约 15 cm。
- 场景：A long oak dining table in a limewashed room, linen runner, two place settings at the far end. Window light from the left, late morning.
- 机位：Eye level of a seated guest, 50 mm, the bowl centered one third from the bottom; table runs away from camera.
- 摆法：Set the six roses first, low and turned outward, so the heads rest just above the rim. Tuck the ranunculus between the roses and let the buds rise a little higher. Walk around the table and turn the bowl until it looks full from every chair.

### 02 Morning Kitchen（Kitchen · White · 16 枝）

- 1 × Real-Touch Tulips · White（12 stems；ASIN B0DQDXM72R）。参考：ssko-v072-tulip-white-set.webp, ssko-v073-tulip-white-detail.webp
- 1 × Flocked Ranunculus · White（4 stems；ASIN B0DTYJ5V1W）。参考：ssko-v072-peony-white-set.webp, ssko-v073-peony-white-detail.webp
- 花器：Clear glass cylinder，高约 20 cm，口径约 9 cm。
- 场景：Honed limestone counter beside a steel-framed window, a stoneware cup and a wooden board in soft focus. Clear morning light.
- 机位：Standing eye level, 50 mm, slight three-quarter angle so the stems show through the glass.
- 摆法：Stand the twelve tulips upright and let them fall open toward the rim. Add the ranunculus at the front, a little lower than the tulips. Keep the stems visible through the glass; they are part of the look.

### 03 Coral Hour（Living · Warm · 10 枝）

- 1 × Classic Roses · Coral（6 stems；ASIN B0DSWT9NMF）。参考：ssko-v072-classic-coral-set.webp, ssko-v073-classic-coral-detail.webp

- 花器：Stone jug，高约 18 cm，口径约 8 cm。
- 场景：Round walnut side table next to a worn leather reading chair, an open book and a cup. Low warm sun from the right, early evening.
- 机位：Seated eye level, 50 mm, jug slightly right of center, chair arm entering the left edge.
- 摆法：Place the roses first and let a few buds lean out toward the chair. Add the ranunculus lower, near the lip of the jug. Leave one side lighter than the other so the arrangement looks gathered, not packed.

- 摆法：Set the eucalyptus first, letting two stems trail down one side. Add the white roses at the center, then the pink roses around them. Step back to the far side of the room and correct the outline from there.

### 05 By the Bed（Bedroom · Blush · 4 枝）

- 1 × Flocked Ranunculus · Baby Pink（4 stems；ASIN B0DTYL1GYT）。参考：ssko-v072-peony-baby-pink-set.webp, ssko-v073-peony-baby-pink-detail.webp
