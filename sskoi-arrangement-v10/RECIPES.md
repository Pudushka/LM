## 每套的文案和摆法

### 01 Blush and Peach

Baby pink Austin roses with light peach ranunculus in a wide-mouth cream pot.

为什么这样配：Two neighboring pinks. The Austin roses form the body; the ranunculus sit between them and repeat the peach at the center of each rose, with their round buds standing a little higher.

1. Place the six roses first, heads turned outward, so they sit a little above the rim.
2. Add the four ranunculus between the roses. Their stems are the longest, so bend them to set the flowers among the roses and let the buds stand above.
3. Turn the pot until one side stands a little higher than the other.

### 02 Coral Hour

Coral classic roses with orange ranunculus in a textured stoneware jar.

为什么这样配：Coral and orange sit next to each other on the color wheel. The roses and their buds set the outline; the orange ranunculus sit among them and warm the center.

1. Place the roses first and let a few buds rise above the open blooms.
2. Add the ranunculus between the roses, bending the stems so the flowers sit at different depths.
3. Leave one side lighter than the other so the arrangement looks gathered, not packed.

### 03 White Garden

White tulips, Austin roses and ranunculus in a wide stoneware crock.

为什么这样配：One color in three textures: tulips gathered low at the rim, cupped Austin roses as the body, layered ranunculus with silvery leaves among and above them.

1. Set the twelve tulips around the rim first. In a crock about 22 cm tall, full-length tulips show about 13 cm above the rim.
2. Place the roses through the center, above the tulips.
3. Add the ranunculus last, between and just above the roses.

### 04 Pink and Green

Green-and-pink tulips with pink Austin roses and pink ranunculus in a sage glazed jar.

为什么这样配：Three pinks at three heights, tied together by the green of the tulips: tulips low at the rim, roses through the middle, ranunculus among and above them.

1. Set the tulips around the rim first, leaning out. In a jar about 22 cm tall they show about 13 cm above the rim.
2. Place the roses in the middle, above the tulips.
3. Tuck the ranunculus between the roses and let their buds stand above.

### 05 Soft White

White classic roses with pink tips and baby pink ranunculus in a speckled stoneware pot.

为什么这样配：The pink edges on the white roses are picked up by the baby pink ranunculus, so the two sets read as one soft color. The rose buds rise highest; the ranunculus sit between the open roses.

1. Set the roses so the open blooms sit near the rim and the buds rise above them.
2. Add the ranunculus between the roses, a little to one side, for an off-center outline.
3. Keep it low and wide on a bedside table, with the open blooms facing the room.

### 06 Late Plum

Burgundy classic roses with dark purple tulips in a brown glazed jar.

为什么这样配：Burgundy and dark purple share their depth. The tulips gather low around the rim; the roses and their buds rise above them.

1. Stand the tulips around the rim first. In a jar about 22 cm tall they show about 13 cm above the rim, below the roses.
2. Place the roses through the middle, open blooms above the tulips and buds highest.
3. Light the table from one side; the dark petals need it.
