## 每套的文案和摆法

### 01 The Long Table

Austin roses and flocked ranunculus, kept low so you can talk across the table.

1. Set the six roses first, low and turned outward, so the heads rest just above the rim.
2. Tuck the ranunculus between the roses and let the buds rise a little higher.
3. Walk around the table and turn the bowl until it looks full from every chair.

### 02 Morning Kitchen

White tulips with white ranunculus in clear glass, for a counter by the window.

1. Stand the twelve tulips upright and let them fall open toward the rim.
2. Add the ranunculus at the front, a little lower than the tulips.
3. Keep the stems visible through the glass; they are part of the look.

### 03 Coral Hour

Coral roses and orange ranunculus in a stone jug beside a reading chair.

1. Place the roses first and let a few buds lean out toward the chair.
2. Add the ranunculus lower, near the lip of the jug.
3. Leave one side lighter than the other so the arrangement looks gathered, not packed.

### 04 Mantel in Blush

Baby pink and white Austin roses with eucalyptus trailing over the edge of the mantel.

1. Set the eucalyptus first, letting two stems trail down one side.
2. Add the white roses at the center, then the pink roses around them.
3. Step back to the far side of the room and correct the outline from there.

### 05 By the Bed

One set of baby pink ranunculus, split between two bud vases.

1. Put two stems in each vase.
2. Set one vase a little forward of the other.
3. Keep the flowers below the lampshade so the light falls on them.

### 06 The Entry

The burgundy gift set in its own paper vase, paired with dark purple tulips in glass.

1. Open the gift box into its standing vase and arrange the eight roses.
2. Stand the tulips upright in the glass vase.
3. Place the two vases a hand’s width apart, the taller one at the back.

### 07 Garden Room

Tulips, Austin roses and ranunculus in pinks and green, loose like a cut-garden bunch.

1. Start with the tulips around the rim, leaning out.
2. Place the roses in the middle at different heights.
3. Fill the gaps with the ranunculus and let the buds stand above the roses.

Burgundy classic roses and purple Austin roses for an evening table.
