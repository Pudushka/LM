## 每套的文案和摆法

### 01 Blush and Peach

Baby pink Austin roses with light peach ranunculus in a cream vase.

为什么这样配：Two neighboring pinks. The Austin roses form the body; the ranunculus, on longer stems, rise above them and repeat the peach at the center of each rose.

1. Place the six roses first, heads turned outward, so they sit a little above the rim.
2. Add the four ranunculus between them; their longer stems carry the flowers and buds above the roses.
3. Turn the vase until one side stands a little higher than the other.
